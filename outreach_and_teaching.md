## Curriculum Development for VT Quantum Computing introductory course
#### [Summer 2021]
- Contributed to the development of coursework for [Hello Quantum World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02868), an introductory course designed to introduce core concepts of quantum information to late high school and early undergraduate students. This course is the foundation of Virginia Tech's new [Minor in QISE](https://vtq.vt.edu/education.html)
- Led a cohort of five high school students during a four-day [summer program](https://sites.google.com/vt.edu/qisesummerschool/home) which utilized these instructional materials
- **Made possible by:** [Dr. Sophia Economou](https://www.phys.vt.edu/About/people/Faculty/sophia-economou.html), [Dr. Edwin Barnes](https://www.phys.vt.edu/About/people/Faculty/edwin-barnes.html), and [Dr. Jamie Sikora](https://sites.google.com/site/jamiesikora/home?authuser=0)
